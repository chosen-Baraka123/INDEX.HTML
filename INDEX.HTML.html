Who we are

New Life Everyday Church Ministries is a community of believers who trust that God makes things new every morning. We gather to worship, study the Bible, pray for one another and serve the people around us.

Whatever you are carrying, you do not have to carry it alone.

What we believe

We believe in one God, the Bible as His Word, salvation through Jesus Christ, and the power of the Holy Spirit to change lives daily.

“His mercies are new every morning.”
Lamentations 3:23
